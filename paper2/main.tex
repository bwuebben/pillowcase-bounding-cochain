\title[Instanton and pillowcase homology of $(-2,3,q)$ pretzel knots]
{Instanton and pillowcase homology of the $(-2,3,q)$ pretzel knots}

\author{Bernd Johannes Wuebben}
\subjclass[2020]{57K18 (primary); 57R58, 53D40, 57K10 (secondary)}
\keywords{Singular instanton homology, pillowcase, Lagrangian Floer homology, pretzel knots, twisted torus
knots, immersed curves, Khovanov homology, Maurer--Cartan elements}
\date{First version: July 27, 2026. This version: October 3, 2026}

\begin{abstract}
For every odd $q\ge3$ the reduced singular instanton homology of the pretzel knot $P(-2,3,q)$ is free
abelian of rank $q+2$, and for $q\ge7$ we record its $\Z/4$-graded ranks. We study two decompositions of
these knots along a Conway sphere. In the first, the tangle that Hedden, Herald and Kirk use for the torus
knot $T(3,5)$ is reglued to the trivial tangle by $(q-5)/2$ Dehn twists. For small holonomy perturbations
and zero bounding cochain we compute the pillowcase Floer homology of this decomposition by hand and show
that it is isomorphic to the instanton homology as a $\Z/4$-graded vector space; the Floer differential is
nonzero for $q\ge9$. This verifies the conjecture of Hedden, Herald and Kirk for a family of hyperbolic
knots outside the two-bridge family, and the same argument applies to the twisted torus knots
$T(3,n;2,m)$ with $n\equiv1,2\pmod 6$. In the second decomposition, Smith's Conway sum, the pairing with
zero bounding cochain has the wrong rank. In the two-arc algebra of Kotelskiy, Watson and Zibrowius, where
the Maurer--Cartan equation is a finite identity, we show that at $q=7$ smoothings of a model of Smith's
curve give Maurer--Cartan elements with the correct pairing, and that a second closure selects one
homotopy class among them, conditionally on the instanton--pillowcase correspondence and a localization
hypothesis. For $q=5,7$ the selected object is the sum of a rational arc and a closed curve with a
two-dimensional local system, and one further term near a corner carries it to the Bar-Natan invariant of
the tangle; no rational closure distinguishes the two. We ask whether the instanton object of the tangle
is its Bar-Natan invariant, and we record observations at $q=7$ and $q\ge11$ that bear on the question.
\end{abstract}
