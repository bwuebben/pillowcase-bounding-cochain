\title{Pillowcase Floer homology of the torus knots $T(3,n)$}

\author{Bernd Johannes Wuebben}
\subjclass[2020]{57K18 (primary); 57R58, 53D40, 57K10 (secondary)}
\keywords{Pillowcase, traceless character varieties, Lagrangian Floer homology, singular instanton homology,
torus knots, S-complexes}
\date{October 2, 2026}

\begin{abstract}
Hedden, Herald and Kirk defined a Lagrangian Floer homology in the pillowcase from a tangle decomposition of
a knot and conjectured that, for a suitable decomposition, it recovers reduced singular instanton homology.
We compute this homology for every torus knot $T(3,n)$, using their decomposition. For all sufficiently small
holonomy perturbations in an explicit open cone, the immersed curves are restricted and the complex has
$1+|\sigma|$ generators, where $\sigma$ is the signature. There is no differential when $n\equiv1,2\pmod 6$ and
exactly one, to the generator of the abelian representation, when $n\equiv4,5\pmod 6$. The $\mathbb Z/4$-graded
homology has the graded dimensions of reduced instanton homology. This verifies the conjecture of Hedden,
Herald and Kirk for this infinite family, and shows that the pillowcase complex has the generators and the
differential predicted by Daemi and Scaduto's S-complex. The proof rests on an explicit description of the
traceless character variety and the strict monotonicity of an angle function along its components; three
inequalities are verified by interval arithmetic. For a different decomposition of $T(3,4)$ in the same family, a numerical computation gives
homology of rank $7$ rather than $5$ with zero bounding cochain.
\end{abstract}
